Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

CashStack is an app by {{business}} (ABN {{abn}}). This policy says what personal information CashStack collects, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. Bank data you share through the Consumer Data Right has its own rules, which our CDR policy covers.

What we collect

  • From Sign in with Apple: an identifier Apple gives us for you, and an email address. Apple lets you hide your real email, in which case we get a private relay address. We don't ask for your name.
  • About each sign-in: the kind of device (for example, "iPhone") and when you signed in, so we can tell your sign-ins apart and end them.
  • From your bank, if you connect one: account names, the last four digits of account numbers, and balances. We read these when you open the app and don't store them. See the CDR policy.
  • Crash and freeze reports: when CashStack crashes or freezes, your iPhone gives it a technical report: what the app was doing, the app and iOS versions, and the iPhone model. CashStack sends it to us without your sign-in or anything else that identifies you, so we can fix the problem.
  • A record of what happened: when you signed in, connected or stopped sharing a bank, or deleted your account. These records hold identifiers and times, never balances or account details.

Some things stay on your phone and are never sent to us: whether you use Face ID, which account fills your wallet, and everything you do with the pile, the till and the jars.

We don't collect your bank login, your transactions, your location or your contacts. CashStack has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Why we collect it

Only to run CashStack: to sign you in, to keep your bank connection working, to show your balance, and to keep a record that lets us answer questions about your account and meet our Consumer Data Right obligations. We may email you about your account or your data sharing. We don't sell personal information or use it for marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Basiq connects CashStack to your bank under the Consumer Data Right. When you connect a bank we give Basiq your email address so it can set you up. Basiq's own privacy and CDR policies are at basiq.io.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple. When you delete your account we tell Apple to end CashStack's access to your Apple ID.
  • Fly.io hosts our servers and database, in Sydney.

We share personal information with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Where it's kept

Our servers and database are in Sydney, Australia. [[Fly.io is a United States company. Confirm with Fly.io whether its staff can reach data from outside Australia, and name those countries here if so.]]

How we keep it safe

Connections are encrypted. Sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes, and the token Apple gives us to end your sign-in is encrypted. On your phone, your session is kept in the Keychain and, if you choose, behind Face ID. Nobody at CashStack can see your bank password, because we never receive it.

How long we keep it

Your account, email address and sign-ins are kept until you delete your account. Sign-ins you stop using expire after 60 days. Crash and freeze reports are deleted after 90 days. The record of what happened is kept for [[six years, the period the Consumer Data Right sets for keeping records. Confirm this with Basiq]] after your account is deleted. It holds identifiers and times only.

Your choices

  • See or correct what we hold about you: email {{email}}.
  • Stop sharing a bank: in CashStack, tap the account name at the top, then Stop sharing. More ways to stop sharing.
  • Delete everything: in CashStack, tap the account name at the top, then Delete my account and data. It takes effect straight away.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal information, email {{email}}. We'll reply within [[30 days]]. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992. For complaints about bank data shared through the Consumer Data Right, see the CDR policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information, we'll tell you in the app first.
