Help and support

Email {{email}} and a person will reply, usually within [[two business days]]. It helps to say which version of CashStack you have. It's at the bottom of the accounts screen: tap the account name at the top of the app.

Please don't send your bank login, account numbers or screenshots of your bank. We'll never ask for them.

Can CashStack move my money?

No. It can only see balances you choose to share. Paying at the till and dropping coins in the jars are practice, and nothing leaves your bank.

Why doesn't my balance match my bank?

Banks update the balance they share about once a day. To fetch the latest, tap the account name at the top, then Refresh balances. If you've been practising at the till, Undo practice puts the pile back to your real balance.

My bank isn't listed, or won't connect

CashStack can connect to banks that take part in the Consumer Data Right. If your bank does and it still won't connect, try again later, as banks sometimes go down for maintenance. If it keeps failing, email us and say which bank.

A bank says it "Needs reconnecting"

Your consent has ended or your bank has reset the connection. Tap the account name at the top and connect the same bank again.

How do I stop sharing my bank data?

Tap the account name at the top, then Stop sharing next to the bank. Other ways to stop sharing.

Face ID won't let me in

On the lock screen, tap Sign out, then sign in again with Apple. Your bank connections are still there, so if CashStack asks you to connect a bank, tap Do this later.

How do I delete my account?

Tap the account name at the top, then Delete my account and data. This disconnects every bank, deletes the data Basiq holds for CashStack and deletes your CashStack account. It can't be undone.
