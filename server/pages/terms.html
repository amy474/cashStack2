What CashStack is

CashStack shows the balance of a bank account you choose as notes and coins. It is read-only: it can see balances you share, but it can't move money, make payments or see your bank login.

  • Paying at the till is practice. Nothing leaves your bank and nobody is paid.
  • The jars are practice too. Coins dropped in the Invest, Donate and Save jars don't buy investments, reach a charity or move between accounts. The funds and charities shown are examples, not recommendations.
  • CashStack isn't a bank and doesn't give financial advice. Nothing in it takes account of your circumstances.

Your account

You sign in with your Apple ID and must be at least [[18]] to use CashStack with a real bank. Keep your phone locked and your Apple ID secure, because anyone who can open CashStack on your phone can see your balance. Turning on Face ID in CashStack helps.

Your bank data

Balances come from your bank through Basiq, under the Consumer Data Right. They're only as current as your bank makes them, which can be up to a day old, so check your bank before relying on a figure. How we handle that data is in our CDR policy and privacy policy. You can stop sharing at any time.
